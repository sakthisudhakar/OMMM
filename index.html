Who We Are

OMMM Detailing Solutions Pvt Limited is an engineering, professional services, technology, and consultancy company focused on delivering accurate, efficient, and reliable solutions for modern projects.

Under the brand OMMM — Oversis Metal Modeling Masters, we bring together engineering expertise and technology-driven solutions to support our clients from project planning to execution.

Our commitment is built on precision, quality, innovation, and client satisfaction, with a vision to create long-term value through dependable and professional services.

How We Work

A clear workflow from project enquiry to final delivery.

01

Project Enquiry

We review your scope, drawings, standards, deliverables and target schedule.

02

Scope & Planning

The team confirms requirements, assigns resources and plans the detailing or engineering workflow.

03

Detailing & Coordination

2D detailing, 3D/BIM modeling, estimation or engineering work is developed to the agreed project requirements.

04

Internal QA/QC

Deliverables are checked for accuracy, coordination, clarity and compliance before issue.

05

Delivery & Support

Final files are issued on schedule, with revision coordination and ongoing project support when required.
